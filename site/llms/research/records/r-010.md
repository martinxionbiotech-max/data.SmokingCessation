## Main question

What does the current evidence say about how to help people quit smoking?

## Main findings

Smoking cessation benefits health at any age; combination approaches (medication plus counseling) are most effective; quitlines, NRT, varenicline and bupropion all have strong evidence.

## Limitations

US-focused; guidelines evolve as new evidence appears.
