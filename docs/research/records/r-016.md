## Main question

Is cytisine more effective than placebo for smoking cessation?

## Main findings

Cytisine significantly increased 12-month continuous abstinence versus placebo (8.4% vs 2.4%).

## Limitations

Single country/center; low absolute quit rates; cytisine was inexpensive relative to alternatives.

## Why this record matters

Landmark trial establishing cytisine efficacy; foundation for subsequent cytisine-vs-NRT comparisons.
