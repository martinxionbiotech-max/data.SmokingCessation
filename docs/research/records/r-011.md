## Main question

What are the global health impacts of tobacco, and what works for cessation?

## Main findings

Tobacco kills more than 8 million people annually; within 20 minutes of quitting heart rate drops, and longer-term risks decline substantially over years. WHO recommends population-level cessation support.

## Limitations

Aggregated global figures; country-level variation.
