## Main question

What does NICE recommend for smoking cessation support in clinical practice?

## Main findings

Offer combination NRT, varenicline or bupropion alongside behavioral support; e-cigarettes can be considered as a stop-smoking aid within local regulations; support should be person-centered and repeated.

## Limitations

UK healthcare context; regulatory status of medications varies internationally.
