## Main question

Do nicotine e-cigarettes help people quit smoking, and are they safe for this purpose?

## Main findings

High-certainty evidence that nicotine e-cigarettes increase quit rates compared with NRT; moderate-certainty evidence that they increase quit rates compared with non-nicotine e-cigarettes and behavioural/no support. Serious adverse effects were rare.

## Limitations

Long-term safety data remain limited; unwanted-effects evidence often low certainty; review is living and updates as new trials publish.

## Why this record matters

Core evidence on e-cigarettes as cessation aids; updates the 2021 record R-009.
