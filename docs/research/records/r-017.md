## Main question

Is cytisine at least as effective as NRT for smoking cessation?

## Main findings

At 1 month, continuous abstinence was higher with cytisine (40% vs 31%); at 6 months the difference narrowed (22% vs 15%) and was not statistically significant. Cytisine was far cheaper than NRT.

## Limitations

Open-label design; quit rates self-reported with some biochemical validation.

## Why this record matters

Key comparative trial for cytisine vs NRT; informs the cost-effectiveness debate.
