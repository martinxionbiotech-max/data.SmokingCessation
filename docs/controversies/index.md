# Controversies

Topics where the published evidence genuinely conflicts, is contested, or remains insufficient. This section presents positions as they are actually held — by named guidelines and studies — without taking sides. Controversies are not manufactured: each topic here has real divergence in the literature or among major guideline bodies.

## Current topics

1. **[E-cigarettes for smoking cessation](e-cigarettes.md)** — Cochrane finds high-certainty evidence they help people quit; WHO and USPSTF decline to recommend them; NICE explicitly allows offering them. Long-term safety unknown.
2. **[Gradual reduction vs abrupt quitting](gradual-vs-abrupt.md)** — the Cochrane evidence says supported gradual and abrupt quitting produce similar quit rates; community experience strongly favors cold turkey; the public debate persists.
3. **[Dual use (smoking + vaping)](dual-use.md)** — is it a bridge to quitting or a trap that sustains smoking?
4. **[Medication comparisons](medication-comparisons.md)** — varenicline vs cytisine vs NRT combinations: what the network meta-analyses actually say.

## Reading rules for this section

- Every position is attributed and dated.
- "Evidence supporting" and "evidence against / limitations" are listed separately.
- Where guidelines disagree, the disagreement is stated flatly — the platform does not arbitrate.
- This is not a list of "both sides" of settled questions. Settled questions (e.g., whether smoking causes cancer) are not controversies.
