# Research Database

Structured records of smoking cessation research. Every record captures:
title, authors, year, journal, DOI/PMID (when available), research type,
population, sample size, method, main question, main findings, limitations,
evidence strength, clinical relevance, related topics and last-verified date.

**Research types:** Systematic Review · Meta-analysis · RCT · Clinical Guideline ·
Cohort Study · Observational Study · Review · Public Health Report.

The first batch of verified records is being compiled. Each record is checked
against its primary source before publication here.
