## Main question

How do the main cessation medications compare with each other?

## Main findings

Combination NRT and varenicline rank among the most effective options, followed by single-form NRT and bupropion, all clearly better than placebo.

## Limitations

Network meta-analysis assumptions; trial populations may differ from typical smokers.
