## Main question

Do cessation medications increase the risk of moderate-to-severe neuropsychiatric adverse events?

## Main findings

No significant increase in moderate-to-severe neuropsychiatric adverse events with varenicline or bupropion versus placebo or patch; varenicline was more effective than placebo, bupropion and patch for continuous abstinence.

## Limitations

Excluded some unstable psychiatric conditions; sponsored trial designed with FDA/EMA.

## Why this record matters

The key modern safety trial that underpinned removal of the varenicline black-box warning.
