## Main question

Does nicotine replacement therapy (NRT) help people quit smoking?

## Main findings

NRT in any form (gum, patch, lozenge, inhaler, spray) increases the rate of quitting by roughly 50% to 70% compared with placebo or no medication, regardless of the setting.

## Limitations

Absolute quit rates remain modest; most participants do not succeed even with NRT.
