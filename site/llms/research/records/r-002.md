## Main question

How effective are varenicline and cytisine for smoking cessation?

## Main findings

Varenicline roughly doubles to triples the odds of quitting compared with placebo in clinical trials. Cytisine also increases quit rates compared with placebo.

## Limitations

Trials mostly exclude people with unstable psychiatric conditions; post-marketing safety discussions continue.
