## Main question

Are nicotine e-cigarettes more effective than conventional NRT for quitting smoking?

## Main findings

One-year abstinence was 18.0% in the e-cigarette group versus 9.9% in the NRT group. E-cigarette users reported less severe urges and more throat/mouth irritation.

## Limitations

Products and setting (UK stop-smoking service with behavioral support) may limit generalizability; long-term safety remains open.
