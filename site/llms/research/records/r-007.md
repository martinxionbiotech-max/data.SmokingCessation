## Main question

Which withdrawal symptoms are valid and how long do they last?

## Main findings

Valid withdrawal symptoms include craving, irritability, restlessness, difficulty concentrating, depressed mood, insomnia, and increased appetite. Most symptoms peak in the first week and resolve within 2–4 weeks; craving and increased appetite can persist longer.

## Limitations

Derived largely from clinical trial data; individual variation is substantial.
