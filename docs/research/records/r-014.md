## Main question

Which behavioural and pharmacological interventions should health systems offer adults who want to quit tobacco?

## Main findings

Recommends a combination of behavioural support and pharmacotherapy; recommends varenicline, NRT, bupropion and cytisine as effective medications; recommends brief advice and digital tools; covers smokeless tobacco users.

## Limitations

Implementation depends on national regulatory access and affordability; e-cigarette recommendations are limited by the current evidence base.
