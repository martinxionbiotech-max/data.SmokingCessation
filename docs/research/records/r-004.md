## Main question

Is quitting abruptly more effective than cutting down gradually before quitting?

## Main findings

People who quit abruptly were more likely to achieve abstinence than those who reduced gradually first. Both approaches produced some successful quitters.

## Limitations

Trials varied in how reduction was defined and supported; participants were treatment-seekers.
